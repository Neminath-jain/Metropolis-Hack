WebAuthn Passkey Quickstart

Grant AI agents bounded, revocable trading authority with biometric passkeys verified onchain via Monad's P256 RIP-7212 precompile.

1
Create Hardware-Backed Passkey

Invokes navigator.credentials.create() to generate a WebAuthn P256 keypair. The public key is extracted from the COSE attestation and hashed to derive a deterministic onchain userId.

2
Register Passkey Onchain

Submits registerPasskey(qx, qy) to AgentGuard on Monad testnet via the gasless relayer. Stores the public key coordinates for future assertion verification.

3
Authorize Trading Policy with Passkey

Constructs a delegation policy for the reference AI agent (2 WMON per-trade, 10 WMON daily). Computes the challenge hash, requests a biometric assertion via signWithPasskey(), normalizes to low-s, and submits gaslessly.

4
Verify Active Session State

Queries AgentGuard.getSession(userId, agentAddress) on Monad testnet to verify the passkey-authorized delegation is active.
